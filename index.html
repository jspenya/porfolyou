How I work

Hey there 👋

I’m Steph, husband to an amazing wife. I’ve been a Ruby on Rails developer since 2018. Somewhere along the way, I started thinking more about why I do this work.

I used to think mostly about what work did for me: my experience, my next opportunity, my paycheck. I’m grateful it provides for my family. But I’m learning to ask, how can I help this business grow, and am I putting love into what I build?

In a story retold by Joe Byerly, President Kennedy asks a NASA janitor what he does. “I’m helping put a man on the moon,” he replies.

There’s a verse in the Bible that reminds me why love matters in the work I do:

“If I have the gift of prophecy and can fathom all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have a faith that can move mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.”

1 Corinthians 13:2 NIV

God comes first, then my family, then my career. I’m learning to keep that order while bringing love and care to the work entrusted to me.

Blessings,
Steph
