Jubilee

Creator and developer

Personal finance software should lower the temperature, not add more noise.

i built jubilee because i wanted to know where my money was going—what was quietly adding up, where i was spending too much, and what i could do differently. having a clearer picture of my finances helps me be wiser with what i have.

  • Ruby on Rails with Hotwire and Stimulus
  • Product direction, interface design, and deployment
  • A deliberately quiet workflow for recurring money decisions

Pickle

Creator and developer

find a place to play without checking every venue one by one.

i built pickle to bring pickleball court availability in cagayan de oro into one search. choose when you want to play, see the matching slots, then head to the venue’s own booking platform to reserve a court.

  • Search availability across venues by date and preferred time
  • See matching court slots and when availability was checked
  • Complete the booking directly with the venue

Fitness

Creator and developer

a place to keep track of food, training, and progress.

i built fitness to bring the everyday pieces of looking after my health together. it keeps meal logging, workout plans, and weigh-ins in one place, with a daily view to help me keep track.

  • Log meals and nutrition alongside daily check-ins
  • Plan the week and record workouts with reusable templates
  • Track weigh-ins and weight goals over time
